## Full description

Inklyne is the private, easy way to edit PDFs on your phone.

Change the text in any PDF: tap a line, type, done. Sign contracts in seconds, highlight what matters, redact what
shouldn't be seen, and export a real PDF you can share anywhere.

EDIT
• Edit existing text right on the page
• Add text, whiteout, and permanent redactions

ANNOTATE
• Highlight, draw, shapes, arrows, and eraser
• Full undo and redo

SIGN & INSERT
• Type, draw, or upload your signature
• Add logos and photos

ORGANIZE
• Reorder, rotate, duplicate, and delete pages
• Merge PDFs and extract pages
• Remove watermarks that are marked as watermarks

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your documents are opened, edited, and exported on your device. Your account only stores names, page counts, and
dates — never the file itself. Use it as a guest, or create a free account to keep your document activity.
